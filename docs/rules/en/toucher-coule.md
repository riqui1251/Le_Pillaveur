# Rules of Hit & Sunk

Le Pillaveur's battleship, reinvented **in teams** and playable free online with 1 to 8 players — solo against the enemy fleet or in 2v2, 3v3, 4v4, with empty seats taken by bots.

## The idea

Two teams place their ships in secret on their grid, then take turns firing at each other. Hit… sunk!

## How a game plays out

1. **Placement**: each team arranges its ships on its grid (horizontal or vertical, no overlapping).
2. **The battle**: the teams fire in turn by picking a cell on the enemy grid.
3. **Hit / Splash / Sunk**: every shot is announced to everyone.
4. The first team to sink the ENTIRE enemy fleet wins.

## Team play

Coordinate your shots over voice chat: a smart search grid beats firing at random. A player away for more than 3 minutes is replaced by a bot — the game never gets stuck.

## Victory

Wipe out the enemy fleet before it wipes out yours.

## Tips

- After a "Hit", test the four adjacent cells.
- Space out your search shots in a checkerboard pattern: most ships are at least 2 cells long.
- Don't place your ships side by side: one sunk ship won't give away a second one.

## Drinking mode

A sunk ship calls for a toast (so to speak — soft mode removes every counter).

## Frequently asked questions

### Can you play without a game master?

Yes. Placement is checked by the server, every shot is announced to everyone, sunk ships are detected automatically. Enemy positions never leave the server until they're hit.

### Can you play remotely?

Yes, and only online: each team has its secret grid on its phones. Voice chat is there to coordinate shots.

### Can you play without alcohol?

Yes. Hit & Sunk is part of the site's alcohol-free mode: no counter, just the fleet.

### Can you play alone?

Yes. In 1v1, alone at the table, a bot commands the enemy fleet — and in every mode, bots fill the empty seats on each team. A bot places its ships and fires all by itself.

### How long does a game last?

An 8×8 grid and three ships per player in 1v1: 5 to 10 minutes. A 14×14 grid in 4v4: 20 to 30 minutes. A hit means you shoot again, so endgames speed up.

### With 1 to 8 players, how are teams split?

The host picks the mode: 1v1 (8×8 grid, ships of 4, 3 and 2 cells per player), 2v2 (10×10), 3v3 (12×12) or 4v4 (14×14) — from 2v2 up, each player places one 4-cell ship and one 3-cell ship. Teams are picked in the lobby, undecided players are assigned, and bots fill the rest.
