# Rules of Crobard

One player draws, everyone else races to guess — Le Pillaveur's drawing game, free and online for 3 to 16 players.

## How it works

Each round, one player gets a secret word and **draws it live** on their phone. Everyone else watches the lines appear in real time and fires off guesses: the fastest score the most points.

## How a round plays out

1. **The word** shows up on the artist's screen (and only theirs).
2. **The drawing** appears live, visible to the whole table (and on the TV if it's plugged in).
3. **The guesses** fly in — the faster you find it, the more you score.
4. The artist also scores when their masterpiece gets guessed. Then the brush passes to the next player: one artist per round.

## Winning

Highest points total at the end of the rounds picked by the host (6, 8 or 10) — at a big table, not everyone necessarily gets to draw (see below).

## Tips

- Draw the CONCEPT before the details: a silhouette people recognize in 5 seconds beats a masterpiece in 60.
- Arrows and breaking things into scenes are your friends.
- Guessers: guess wide and fast, every attempt is free.

## The drinking mode

The game only counts points, but tradition says the artist whose drawing nobody guesses has to drink — and honestly, sometimes they deserve it. Soft mode available.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can you play without a game master?

Yes. The game offers the artist three words, times the round, validates correct answers and counts the points. Nothing to referee.

### Can you play remotely?

Yes, and only online: the drawing appears live for everyone, and on the TV if it's plugged in. Voice chat is for banter — not for feeding answers.

### Can you play alcohol-free?

Yes. Crobard is part of the site's Alcohol-free mode: points are all you need.

### Can you play solo, with bots?

You can, to get a feel for the screen, but there's no real game in it: a bot doesn't draw (its round is cut to a few seconds) and doesn't guess. Bots are there to fill a small table — Crobard needs real eyes.

### How long does a game last?

10 seconds to pick a word, 80 to draw — less if everyone has guessed it. The host picks 6, 8 or 10 rounds: count on 10 to 15 minutes.

### With 12 players, does everyone draw?

Each round has a single artist: with 8 rounds and 12 players, four people never get the brush. Above 10 players, pick 10 rounds — or split into two tables.
