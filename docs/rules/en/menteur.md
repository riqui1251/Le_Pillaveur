# Rules of Liar's Dice

The bidding-and-bluffing game played under a cup — in the spirit of Perudo, playable **free online with 2 to 6 players**.

## The idea

Each player shakes their dice **hidden under their cup**: only they can see them. Taking turns, you bid on what the WHOLE table holds ("there are at least four 3s"). Don't believe it? Shout **"LIAR!"**.

## How a game plays out

1. **The roll**: everyone looks at their dice in secret.
2. **The bidding**: each player must raise the bid (more dice, or a higher value) — or call out the previous player.
3. **LIAR!**: all the cups come up. If the bid held, the accuser loses a die; if not, the liar pays for it.
4. **Reveal**: all the cups stay open for a few seconds, long
   enough for the table to see the verdict, then the dice are rolled again.
5. A player with no dice left is eliminated.

## Variants included

- **Palifico**: when a player goes down to a single die, the round is played with a locked face.
- **Calza**: bet that the current bid is EXACTLY right to win back a die.

## Victory

The last player with dice wins the table.

## Tips

- Your own dice are your only certainty: bid around what you're holding.
- Stats help: on 20 dice, each face shows up a little over 3 times on average — and the 1s, the "Pillaveurs", are wild and count for every face (except in a Palifico round, or when the bid is on 1s). So for a face from 2 to 6, bank on 6 or 7 dice.

## Drinking mode

**The bill climbs with every die lost**: on the first die lost, the unlucky player
drinks **1 sip**; on the second, **2**; on the third, **3**… In other words,
they drink **as many sips as dice they've already lost**, every time. Losing
all five dice therefore costs 15 sips in total — and elimination is celebrated with a symbolic
bottoms up.

A **successful Calza** makes nobody drink (and wins back a die). Soft mode: just
the dice, no counter.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can you play without a game master?

Yes. The server rolls the dice under each cup, checks that every bid
really raises the last one, and lifts all the cups on "Liar!". Nobody
counts by hand.

### Can you play remotely?

Yes, and only online: the dice stay hidden on each phone,
which is impossible on a single screen. Voice chat brings the bar-counter atmosphere.

### Can you play without alcohol?

Yes. Liar's Dice is part of the site's alcohol-free mode: just the dice,
no sip counter.

### Can you play alone, with bots?

Yes. "Try it with bots" opens a private table with one bot — the host can
add up to five. The bots bid and call liars, and your friends can
join along the way.

### How long does a game last?

There's no timer per turn, but a player who stops acting for 3 minutes is
replaced by a bot: the table never gets stuck. A 4-player game
(20 dice) lasts 10 to 15 minutes; with 2, it's a 5-minute duel.

### Does it work with 2 players?

Yes: 5 dice against 5, it's a real duel. But it's with 4, 5 or 6 that the
statistics lie best.
