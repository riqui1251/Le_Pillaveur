# Rules of 1220

A **12**-sided die, a **20**-sided die, and four bets to set before the roll: 1220 is Le Pillaveur's mathematically sneaky drinking game. Free, online, for 2 to 16 players.

## How it works

Before each roll, every player sets their bets:

- the **parity** of the sum (even or odd);
- the **range** of the sum (2-10, 11-20 or 21-30);
- a "**Makes you drink**" number;
- a "**Give a drink**" number (different from the first).

## How a roll plays out

1. **The bets**: everyone sets up their four bets on their phone.
2. **The roll**: anyone can roll both dice — the result shows up for everyone.
3. **The settlement**:
   - sum equals your "Makes you drink" → you drink;
   - sum equals your "Give a drink" → you hand out sips;
   - right parity or right range → you hand out too;
   - a number showing on just ONE die counts for half — stack the combos and soak the table!
4. Roll again, tweak your bets, and watch the table come alive.

## Winning

1220 is a party game: no podium, just bets landing and combos that make the table roar. The online leaderboard still counts the games.

## Tips

- The most frequent sum hovers around 16-17: place your ranges knowing that.
- A "Give a drink" on a middle number (10-16) makes your rolls pay off better.

## The drinking mode

It's literally the theme of the game — and as everywhere on the site, a "sip" is an abstract game unit: everyone puts whatever they like in their glass. 1220 isn't part of the site's Alcohol-free mode: without betting on who drinks, there's nothing left to bet on.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can you play without a game master?

Yes. Everyone sets their bets on their phone, anyone rolls the dice, and the server does the math for the whole table. Nobody has to keep score.

### Can you play remotely?

Yes: an online table, a code or QR to share, and voice chat to react to the rolls. 1220 also works locally, on a single phone passed from hand to hand.

### Can you play alcohol-free?

Not online: 1220 isn't in the site's Alcohol-free catalog, since the whole game is about betting on who drinks. Nothing stops you from filling the glasses with water or soda — a sip is just a unit.

### Can you play solo, with bots?

Yes. "Try it with bots" opens a private table that a bot fills, and your friends can join mid-game.

### How long does a game last?

Three minutes of setup at most (when time's up, anyone not ready is marked ready), then as many rolls as the table wants: the game ends when a player calls it. Count on 10 to 20 minutes for a good run.

### Does it work with 2 players?

Yes, but combos that soak the table are less fun with two. 1220 really comes into its own with 4 or 5 different bets on the same roll, and holds up to 16.
