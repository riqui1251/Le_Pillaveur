# Rules of Who's the Spy?

Everyone knows the location… except one person. Flush out **the spy** before they figure out where they are. Free, online, for 3 to 16 players.

## The idea

A secret location is dealt to every player — except the spy, who only gets "You are the spy". By asking each other questions, the crew hunts for the intruder, while the spy tries to guess the location from the answers.

## How a game plays out

1. **The deal**: everyone sees the location on their phone (or their spy status).
2. **The questions**: out loud (voice chat) or in writing, you quiz each other: "Do you go there often?", "Is the food good there?" — precise enough to test, vague enough not to give the location away.
3. **The public accusation**: when a player is convinced, they accuse. The crew decides.
4. **The reveal**: an unmasked spy can still win by guessing the location.

## Victory

- **The crew wins** by unmasking the spy (who fails to find the location).
- **The spy wins** if they stay hidden, or if they guess the location.

## Tips

- Answer quick as a flash: hesitation gives you away.
- Spy: recycle the other players' words in your answers.
- Crew: a question that's too precise helps the spy more than you.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can you play without a game master?

Yes. The game draws the location, secretly picks the spy, runs the timer and handles the accusation: 15 seconds to rally the table, majority reached → reveal. Nothing to referee.

### Can you play remotely?

Yes, and only online: everyone sees their location (or their spy status) on their phone. Voice chat is the heart of the game — the questions are asked out loud.

### Can you play without alcohol?

Yes. Who's the Spy? is part of the site's alcohol-free mode.

### Can you play alone, with bots?

Yes, to discover the mechanics ("Try it with bots"), but the spy is flushed out through conversation and a bot doesn't chat. Bots fill a table, they don't bring it to life: you need real voices.

### How long does a game last?

The host sets the discussion (3, 5 or 7 minutes per round) and the number of rounds to win (3, 5 or 7). A standard game — 5 minutes, 3 winning rounds — lasts 15 to 25 minutes. Time runs out with no accusation? The spy wins the round.

### Does it work with 3 players?

Yes: two votes are then enough to settle an accusation. With 8 or more, the spy blends into the crowd and the questions go round for longer — it's the tensest format, up to 16.
