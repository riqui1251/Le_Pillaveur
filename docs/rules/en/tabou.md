# Rules of Taboo Voice

Get your team to guess a word **without saying the forbidden words** — over voice chat, in teams. Free, online, for 4 to 16 players.

## The idea

Two teams face off. Each round, a describer sees a word to get THEIR team to guess… with a list of **taboo** words they're not allowed to say.

## How a game plays out

1. **The teams** form in the lobby (A vs B).
2. **The describer** sees the word + its taboos on their phone; their team listens on voice chat.
3. **The guesses fly** for 60 seconds: word found = point, on to the next one, the clock keeps running.
4. Too hard? You can pass. A taboo word said out loud cancels the word — it's the opposing team that calls it.
5. Everyone takes a turn as describer; the first team to reach the target score wins.

## Victory

The first team to reach the target score chosen by the host (15, 20 or 25 words guessed).

## Tips

- Pass quickly on words that get stuck: the clock is your real opponent.
- Describe by use ("you use it to…") when the definition is taboo.
- The listening team should fire off guesses nonstop: every silence costs.

## Drinking mode

The game only counts points, but tradition says the team that ends its round without a single word found drinks up. Soft mode: the score is all the happiness you need.

## Variants and player counts

- **4 players — 2 vs 2**: each player describes every other round. An
  intense format, where the duo that knows each other best wins.
- **6 to 10 players**: three to five pairs of ears per team — the guesses
  fly, the describer just has to confirm.
- **12 to 16 players**: big teams; raise the target score to 25 so
  everyone gets to describe at least once.
- **Uneven teams**: possible, each team just needs two
  players. Everyone describes once per lap of the table: the bigger team
  therefore gets more rounds.
- **With bots**: a bot doesn't describe — its round is cut to a few
  seconds — and doesn't guess. Count on at least two humans per team.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can you play without a game master?

Yes. The game draws the words, runs the clock and counts the points; it's
the opposing team that listens out for taboo words and hits "TABOO!". No
outside referee.

### Can you play remotely?

Yes, and only online: the describer talks on voice chat, their team listens
and guesses out loud. No mic, no Taboo.

### Can you play without alcohol?

Yes. Taboo Voice is part of the site's alcohol-free mode: the score
is enough.

### Can you play alone, with bots?

No, not really: a bot doesn't describe and doesn't guess. You need at least two
humans per team for the game to come alive.

### How long does a game last?

60 seconds per round, three to four words guessed on average: a target score
of 20 plays out in 15 to 20 minutes.

### Do you need an account?

No: a nickname is enough, the guest account is created on the spot, no email needed.
