# Rules of Drawn Telephone

The telephone game, drawing edition: a sentence becomes a sketch, the sketch turns back into a sentence… and by the end, nobody recognizes a thing. Free, online, for 3 to 8 players.

## How it works

Each player starts a **chain** with a sentence of their own. The chains then travel from player to player, alternating: I draw the sentence I received → the next player describes my drawing → the next one draws their description…

## How a game plays out

1. **The starting sentence**: everyone writes whatever they like (be bold, not too simple).
2. **The drawing**: you receive another player's sentence — draw it, no words allowed!
3. **The description**: you receive a drawing — describe what you see.
4. You keep alternating until every chain has gone all the way around the table.
5. **The reveal**: each chain is replayed from start to finish in front of everyone — THIS is the moment of the game. A leader scrolls through the chains (and can go back), and if nobody touches anything, the next one shows up on its own after 45 seconds: the table never waits for a sleepy player.

## Winning

Officially, nobody wins. Unofficially, whoever's chain went the most off the rails won the night.

## Tips

- Starting sentences: an action + an unexpected subject ("a penguin robs a bakery").
- Draw the ACTION, not just the characters.
- Describe what you see, not what you assume.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can you play without a game master?

Yes. The chains travel on their own, with a timer for each step. The only "leader" is whoever scrolls through the chains during the reveal — and if nobody does, it moves on by itself after 45 seconds.

### Can you play remotely?

Yes, and only online: everyone draws and writes on their own phone, without seeing anyone else's chains before the reveal. Voice chat is there so you can laugh together at the end.

### Can you play alcohol-free?

Yes — there isn't a single sip in this game, just fits of laughter. It's part of the site's Alcohol-free mode.

### Can you play solo, with bots?

No: no bots, every link is a human. You need at least three players.

### How long does a game last?

60 seconds to write, 80 to draw, as many steps as there are players, then 45 seconds per chain during the reveal. With 6 players, count on 12 to 15 minutes.

### With 3 or with 8, what's the difference?

With 3, the chains are short (sentence → drawing → sentence) and barely drift; with 8, it's eight chains of eight links — the reveal alone lasts six minutes, and that's where everything goes haywire.
