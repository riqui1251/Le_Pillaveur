# Rules of Mots Codés — clues, teams and the assassin

**Mots Codés** ("Coded Words") is Le Pillaveur's team word-association game: a grid of 25 words, two sides (Gold and Red), and one Spymaster per team who gets their teammates to guess several words with a single clue. Free, 4 to 16 players, with built-in voice chat — the team debate is what makes the game.

## How it works

On the grid, each team owns **its words** (9 for the team that starts, 8 for the other), plus 7 neutral words and **1 assassin**. Only the **Spymasters** see the colors: for everyone else, the grid stays cream until something is revealed.

## The clue

On their turn, the active team's Spymaster gives a clue: **ONE single word and a number** — for example "VINEYARD · 3" to link *grape*, *cellar* and *barrel*. Not allowed: a word still visible on the grid, and phrases. The clue is said out loud on voice chat and locked in the app for the whole table.

## Guessing

Their team debates, then taps the tiles one by one:

- **Their color**: the tile is revealed, the team can keep going (number of guesses = the clue + 1).
- **Neutral or the other team's color**: the tile is revealed and **the turn passes** (and an opposing tile brings the enemy closer to victory!).
- **The assassin**: **instant** defeat for the team that hit it.

The team can also **pass the turn** voluntarily at any time.

## Winning

The first team to have **all its words revealed** wins — even if it's the opponents who reveal its last word by mistake.

## Good to know

- **4 players minimum, 2 per team** (one Spymaster + at least one guesser); up to 16. The first player of each team in the lobby becomes Spymaster.
- Teams are picked in the lobby; the undecided are assigned automatically.
- No bots at launch: a bot can't give a clue. A player who leaves is replaced after 3 minutes (a departed Spymaster hands their role to a teammate).
- TV mode shows the grid big — without ever showing the solution.

## Variants and player counts

The grid doesn't change with the player count (always 25 words: 9, 8, 7 neutrals and the assassin); what changes is the size of the debate.

- **4 players — 2 vs 2**: one Spymaster and a single guesser per side. Quick games, cautious clues: the guesser has nobody to argue with.
- **6 to 8 players**: the ideal format. Two or three guessers per team debate, while the Spymaster suffers in silence.
- **10 to 16 players**: big teams, long debates — pick a spokesperson to tap the tiles, or the guessing timer (2 minutes) runs out before you agree.
- **Uneven teams**: allowed, the only rule is **two humans per side at launch**. A bigger team guesses faster, but its Spymaster has more opinions to satisfy.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can you play without a game master?

Yes. The game draws the grid, the colors stay hidden server-side, and tiles reveal themselves when tapped. Spymasters are players, not referees.

### Can you play remotely?

Yes, and only online: only the Spymasters see the solution, on their own phone. The clue is said on voice chat and locked on the whole table's screen.

### Can you play alcohol-free?

Yes — there isn't a single sip in Mots Codés anyway, just your team's honor. The game is part of the site's Alcohol-free mode.

### Can you play solo, with bots?

No: a bot can't give a clue. You need four humans at launch, two per team. A player who leaves mid-game is replaced after 3 minutes.

### How long does a game last?

90 seconds to give the clue, 2 minutes to guess, every turn. Count on 10 to 20 minutes per grid — less if someone hits the assassin.

### Do you need an account?

No: a nickname is enough, the guest account is created on the spot, no e-mail. You can save it later to keep your stats.
