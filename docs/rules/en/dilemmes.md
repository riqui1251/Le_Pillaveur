# Rules of Dilemmes — secret votes and reveals

**Dilemmes** ("Dilemmas") is Le Pillaveur's icebreaker: "Would you rather", "Never have I ever" and "Who at the table" as secret votes, revealed all at once to the whole table. Free, 3 to 16 players, five-minute games — perfect for warming up the night before the big games.

## How it works

For each card, everyone votes **in secret** from their phone:

- **Would you rather…**: two options, you have to pick a side.
- **Never have I ever…**: "I've done it 😏" or "Never ever".
- **Who at the table…**: you vote for a **player** (never yourself).

Nobody sees the votes until everyone has voted — then it all shows up **at once**: the sides, the percentages, and who voted what.

## The minority drinks

In **With alcohol** mode, the minority side drinks at every reveal. In **Soft** mode, you play the same tame cards without sips — the fun of the reveals stays intact.

## How a game plays out

1. The host picks the number of cards (10, 15 or 20) and starts the table.
2. Each card: 30 seconds to vote (the reveal comes as soon as everyone has voted), then you discover the sides and argue it out.
3. After the last card, the game ends — **no winner, no ranking**: Dilemmes is about the conversations it sparks, not the score.

## Good to know

- **3 players minimum**, up to 16 — and the more of you there are, the better the reveals.
- The card content is **in French** and follows the same tone charter as Sans Filtre.
- Bots vote according to their temperament, the bold one leaning "daring", the wise one leaning "tame" (they're there to fill a small table).
- TV mode shows the card big, and the sides at the reveal.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can you play without a game master?

Yes. The cards follow one another on their own: the reveal drops as soon as everyone has voted, or after 30 seconds. Nobody reads the cards or counts raised hands.

### Can you play remotely?

Yes, and only online: everyone votes in secret on their phone, and the reveal is simultaneous. Voice chat is for the debate that follows each card.

### Can you play alcohol-free?

Yes. Dilemmes is part of the site's Alcohol-free mode: same tame cards, no sips, and the reveals are still there.

### Can you play solo, with bots?

Technically yes — two bots vote according to their temperament — but Dilemmes is a conversation game and a bot has no stories to tell. Bots are there to fill a table of two or three, not to replace it.

### How long does a game last?

30 seconds of voting at most per card, then however long you spend talking about it. With 10, 15 or 20 cards, count on 5 to 15 minutes.

### Does it work with 3 players?

Yes: "Who at the table" is then played between two candidates, since you can't vote for yourself. From 8 on, the percentages really start to say something — and up to 16, the reveal always lands.
