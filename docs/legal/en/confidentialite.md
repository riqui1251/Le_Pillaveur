# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 14 June 2025

## 1. Introduction

This privacy policy describes how **Le Pillaveur** (hereinafter the "Service"), published by **Simon Cozzi**, collects, uses, and protects your personal data in accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the French Data Protection Act (Loi Informatique et Libertés).

**Personal data contact:** lepillaveur@outlook.fr

## 2. Data controller

- **Controller:** Simon Cozzi
- **Address:** available upon request at lepillaveur@outlook.fr

## 3. Data collected

### 3.1 Account data (registration)

When you create an account, we collect:

- email address;
- password (stored as a **cryptographic hash** — never in plain text);
- username (display name).

### 3.2 Game data

If you use an account, we may store:

- game statistics (number of games, wins, losses);
- "sip" counters (abstract playful units, with no link to actual consumption);
- unlocked achievements;
- synchronised player list (local usernames you create).

In **local mode** (without an account), this data remains on your device in the browser's local storage and is not transmitted to the server, unless you subsequently create an account and synchronise.

### 3.3 Technical and usage data

For operation, security, and aggregated statistics, we may collect:

- IP address;
- estimated country (approximate geolocation by IP address);
- device / browser type (simplified user-agent);
- login dates and time spent on the site;
- anonymous visitor identifier (analytics cookie).

### 3.4 User feedback

If you use the feedback form, we may collect:

- your message;
- screenshots you voluntarily attach;
- contact email (optional);
- technical context (page visited, browser).

### 3.5 Cookies

The Service uses the following cookies:

| Cookie | Purpose | Duration |
|--------|---------|----------|
| `lp_session` | Maintaining your logged-in session | Session or configured duration |
| `lp_local_play` | Enabling local mode without an account | Persistent |
| `lp_vid` | Visitor identifier for aggregated statistics | Persistent |
| `lp_age_verified` | Recording your age declaration (18+) | 1 year |
| `lp_locale` | Storing your interface language preference | 1 year |

## 4. Purposes of processing

Your data is processed to:

- create and manage your account;
- authenticate your logins;
- synchronise your players and statistics across devices;
- ensure the security of the Service and prevent abuse;
- moderate accounts (suspension, banning in the event of a violation);
- respond to your support and feedback requests;
- produce aggregated and anonymised audience statistics;
- comply with our legal obligations.

We **do not sell** your personal data to third parties.

Your data may be processed by:

The publisher remains the data controller. No other transfer to third parties is carried out without your consent, except where required by law.

## 7. Retention period

- **Active account:** data retained for as long as the account exists.
- **Deleted account:** deletion or anonymisation within a maximum of **12 months** after the request, unless a longer legal retention obligation applies.
- **Technical logs:** retention limited to **6 months**.
- **Feedback:** retention for up to **24 months** or deletion upon request.
- **Age cookie:** 1 year, renewable upon each validation.

## 8. Your rights

In accordance with the GDPR, you have the following rights:

- **Access:** obtain a copy of your data;
- **Rectification:** correct inaccurate data;
- **Erasure:** request deletion of your data;
- **Restriction:** restrict certain processing;
- **Objection:** object to processing based on legitimate interest;
- **Portability:** receive your data in a structured format (where applicable).

To exercise your rights, contact: lepillaveur@outlook.fr

You may also lodge a complaint with the **CNIL** (www.cnil.fr).

## 9. Security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures:

- hashed passwords (bcrypt);
- secure session cookies;
- HTTP security headers (CSP, etc.);
- restricted administrator access.

No transmission over the Internet is completely secure; we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 10. Minors

The Service is intended for persons aged **18 or over**. We do not knowingly collect personal data from minors. If you believe a minor has submitted data to us, please contact us to request its deletion.
