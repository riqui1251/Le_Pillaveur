# Rules of The Big Bluff

The game where **the wrong answer pays off**: invent the most believable answer, and lure your friends into the trap. Free, online, for 3 to 16 players.

## The idea

A quiz question appears, without its answer choices. Often, nobody is sure of the real one — so **everyone invents one** that's as believable as possible. All the answers (the fakes + the real one) are then shuffled, and everyone votes for the one they think is right.

## How a game plays out

1. **The question** appears for everyone.
2. **The invention**: everyone submits their fake answer within the time limit.
3. **The vote**: all the answers are shown shuffled — find the real one!
4. **The reveal**: points for those who found the real answer AND for those whose bluff caught votes.

## Points

- Finding the real answer scores.
- Every player fooled by YOUR fake answer scores for you too.

## Victory

The best total at the end of the series of questions.

## Tips

- Copy the style of real answers: precise figures, plain wording.
- Too funny = spotted; too flat = ignored. A good bluff is plausible.
- Beware the answer that "sounds too good"… it might be a trap.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can you play without a host?

Yes. The questions come from the game, the answers are shuffled by the server and the votes are counted automatically. Nobody knows the answer in advance… except the game.

### Can you play remotely?

Yes, and only online: everyone on their own phone, a code to share, voice chat optional. The invented answers have to stay secret until the vote — impossible on a single screen.

### Can you play without alcohol?

Yes. The Big Bluff is part of the site's alcohol-free mode: you play for points, nothing else changes.

### Can you play alone, with bots?

Yes. "Try it with bots" starts a private table with two bots that invent their fake answer and vote like everyone else. Your friends can join along the way.

### How long does a game last?

45 seconds to invent, 60 to vote, then the reveal: about two minutes per question. The host picks 6, 8 or 10 questions, so 12 to 20 minutes.

### How many players is best?

From 3 (two bluffs and the real answer) up to 16: the more answers in the mix, the better the real one hides. Beyond 10, allow more time to read all the suggestions.
