# Rules of Purple

Red, black… or purple? The simplest — and sneakiest — card-betting game in the house. Free, online, for 2 to 16 players.

## How it works

On your turn, you bet on the color of the next cards in the deck. Good call: the **pot** of sips grows, and you choose to keep going or pass the turn. Bad call: you drink **the whole pot**, and the turn moves on.

## The bets

- **Red** or **Black**: one card, the right color — the pot gains **1**.
- **Double red** or **Double black**: two cards of the same color — the pot gains **2**.
- **Purple**: two cards, one of each color — the pot gains **2**.
- **Double Purple**: four cards, two mismatched pairs — the pot gains **4**.

## How a turn plays out

1. **The bet**: the active player picks their bet on their phone.
2. **The cards** flip over for everyone at the same time.
3. **Good call**: the pot grows. You **keep going** (new bet, same pot) or you **pass the turn** — the pot follows the next player, hot-potato style.
4. **Bad call**: you drink the entire pot (bet included), it drops back to zero, and the turn moves on.

## The "purple" bet

Purple bets on the mix: one red AND one black in the draw, in any order. Less likely than a single color, it grows the pot faster — it's what gives the game its spice (and its name).

## Winning

No podium: Purple stops when the table decides, and the game shows who drank how much. The online leaderboard still counts the games.

## Tips

- Cards have no memory: a "streak of reds" doesn't make black any more likely.
- Pass the turn when the pot gets heavy — the next player will have to carry it.
- Try purple on a pot that's still empty, not on a pot of six.

## The drinking mode

Purple is all about the pot: each "sip" stays an abstract game unit, and everyone puts whatever they like in their glass. The game isn't part of the site's Alcohol-free mode — without a pot to make someone drink, there's nothing left to bet on.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can you play without a game master?

Yes. The game shuffles the deck, the cards flip on everyone's screen, and the pot counts itself. Nobody has to hold the cards.

### Can you play remotely?

Yes: an online table, a code or QR to share, and voice chat to cheer on the active player. Purple also works locally, on a single phone passed from hand to hand.

### Can you play alcohol-free?

Not online: Purple isn't in the site's Alcohol-free catalog, since the whole game revolves around the pot. Nothing stops you from filling the glasses with water or soda — a sip is just a unit.

### Can you play solo, with bots?

Yes. "Try it with bots" opens a private table that a bot fills: it bets, keeps going and passes the turn like a player. Your friends can join mid-game.

### How long does a game last?

No set end: each bet takes a few seconds, and the table stops whenever it wants. Count on 10 to 20 minutes for a good run. A player who stays idle for 3 minutes is replaced by a bot.

### Does it work with 2 players?

Yes: the pot bounces back and forth, and passing the turn becomes a real poker move. Up to 16, it goes around the table — and watch out for whoever gets it full.
