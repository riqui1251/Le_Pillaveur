# Rules of The Big Pillaveur Quiz

Le Pillaveur's speed quiz: **hundreds of questions**, a buzzer, and points that go to the fastest. Free, online, for 2 to 16 players, each on their own phone — perfect with **TV mode** to show the questions on the big screen.

## The idea

A question appears with several answers. Everyone answers on their phone: **the faster you answer correctly, the more points you score**.

## How a game plays out

1. **The question** appears for everyone at the same time: four answers,
   **15 seconds**.
2. **Everyone buzzes** in their answer — one answer per player, timestamped by the
   server (no cheating on speed).
3. **The reveal** shows the right answer and the standings (6 seconds; it
   can be cut short once the table has had time to read).
4. The questions keep coming — **10, 15 or 20** depending on the host's choice — and the
   total crowns the champion.

## Scoring

- Right answer: **100 points**, plus a **speed bonus of up to 100
  points** (it melts away over the 15 seconds).
- Wrong answer, or no answer: **0 points**.
- Three right answers in a row light up the 🔥 streak (bragging rights only).

## The questions

General knowledge, sport, music, movies and series, food and drinks, the party world — three difficulty levels, refreshed regularly, available in four languages.

## Victory

The best points total at the end of the series wins the game — and a spot on the site's online leaderboard.

## Tips

- A quick hunch often beats slow thinking: the scoring rewards speed.
- Don't buzz at random every time: zero points is unforgiving over the long run.

## Drinking mode

**Every wrong answer costs 2 sips** — and not answering at all counts
as a wrong answer. The sip counter follows each player all the way to the
podium, where the last one finishes their glass. In soft mode, the game is called "Quiz
Party" and the counter disappears.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can you play without a host?

Yes. Questions, timer, marking and podium are automatic: nobody
reads the questions or keeps score. The TV or the phones take care of it.

### Can you play remotely?

Yes, and only online: everyone buzzes on their phone, the server
timestamps. Voice chat is there for trash talk between questions.

### Can you play without alcohol?

Yes. In alcohol-free mode, the game is called "Quiz Party": same questions,
same scoring, no sip counter.

### Can you play alone, against bots?

Yes. "Try it with bots" starts a private table with one bot; the host
can line up to eleven. The bots answer — and get it wrong — just like
players. Your friends can join along the way.

### How long does a game last?

15 seconds per question and 6 for the reveal: 10 questions take about
4 minutes, 20 questions 7 to 8 minutes. The ultimate drinks-time format.

### How many players is best?

2 to 16 players. The podium starts to mean something from 3; from 8 on, plug in the TV so
everyone sees the question big.
