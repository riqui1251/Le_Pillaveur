# Rules of Petit Bac online — letter, STOP and scoring

Le Pillaveur's **Petit Bac** (also called "baccalauréat" — the French Scattergories): one letter, five categories, and the first to fill in their sheet yells **STOP**. Free, 2 to 16 players, each on their own phone — no more cheaters peeking at their neighbor's sheet.

## How it works

1. Each round draws a **letter** (impossible letters like K, W or X are left out) and **five new categories** — First name, Animal, Drink, Fictional character, Monument… drawn from twenty-four, different every round.
2. Everyone writes **at the same time**, in secret, on their phone.
3. The first player to have filled their five boxes **for real** yells **STOP**: the table freezes, and everyone else hands in their sheet as is. STOP has two conditions, checked by the server — all five answers must **start with the right letter** and be at least two characters long, and the round must have run for **at least 20 seconds**: no STOP at second zero with five "aaaaa".

## Scoring (automatic)

- **Unique**, valid answer: **2 points**.
- **Duplicate** answer (two players wrote the same thing, accents and capitals ignored): **1 point** each.
- **Empty** box or an answer that doesn't start with the right letter: **0 points**.

## Contesting

"*Zorglub*, is that even a first name?" — at the reveal, each player can **contest** an answer they think is made up. If the **majority of the other players** contest it too, the answer is thrown out and drops to 0. No referee: the table decides.

**It always takes at least two votes**: at a table of two (or when only one opponent is left in play), nobody can strike out the other's sheet alone — the contest button is simply unavailable.

The scoring grid stays on screen long enough for everyone to read it: the first impatient player can't launch the next round before the others.

## How a game plays out

- The host picks the number of rounds (**3, 5 or 8**) — a different letter every round, never the same one twice.
- 2 minutes max per writing round (but a STOP cuts it short).
- At the end, the player with the **most total points** wins — wins and ranks count toward Le Pillaveur's online leaderboard.

## Good to know

- **2 players minimum**, up to 16 — the duplicate/unique scoring gets really fun from 4 on.
- No bots: Petit Bac relies on real typed answers (a player who leaves is replaced by a blank sheet).
- TV mode shows the letter in giant type, the sheets' progress, then the scoring grid.
- Playable in all four of the site's languages (categories translated).

## Frequently asked questions

### Can you play without a game master?

Yes. The game draws the letter, the server checks the STOP, scoring is automatic, and contests are settled by majority. Nobody reads the sheets out loud.

### Can you play remotely?

Yes, and only online: everyone fills in their sheet on their phone, and the reveal drops at the same time for everyone.

### Can you play alcohol-free?

Yes — there isn't a single sip in Petit Bac, just points. The game is part of the site's Alcohol-free mode.

### Can you play solo, with bots?

No: no bots, you need at least two players. A bot can't come up with "a fruit starting with U".

### How long does a game last?

2 minutes of writing at most per round (often less, a STOP comes fast), then 90 seconds of reading and contesting. With 3, 5 or 8 rounds, count on 10 to 30 minutes.

### Does it work with 2 players?

Yes, but with no contesting possible (it takes two votes to strike an answer): everyone's on their honor. Unique/duplicate scoring gets fun from 4 on, and the table holds up to 16.
